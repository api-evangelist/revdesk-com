RevDesk is the revenue operating platform of Cell Labs, Inc., offering auto and RV dealers a fast lead‑to‑close solution. It qualifies leads using buying signals and helps teams close with personalized video, AI voice agents, SMS outreach, and compliance tools. The platform provides APIs, SDKs, and a CLI for developers to integrate lead management, messaging, phone‑number handling, and webhooks into their workflows.

**Areas:** Company · AutoDealer · LeadManagement · APIPlatform · AI

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for RevDesk: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
